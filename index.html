What does this unlock — and what doesn't it?

This tool removes <sheetProtection ... /> from protected worksheet XML inside a standard .xlsx archive. It does not crack encrypted, password-to-open workbooks, remove workbook-structure protection, or support legacy .xls files. Files with digital signatures may lose signature validity when repackaged. Use it only for workbooks you are permitted to modify.
